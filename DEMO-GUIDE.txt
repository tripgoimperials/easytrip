EASYTRIP DEMONSTRATION GUIDE

1. Start Apache and MySQL in XAMPP.
2. Open phpMyAdmin and create/import EasyTrip SQL scripts 00-09 in order.
   If upgrading an existing EasyTrip database, also run 10 and 11 once.
3. Open http://localhost/EasyTrip/
4. Register a passenger and show the new row in phpMyAdmin > easytrip > users.
5. Log in and show that the dashboard/profile displays the registered passenger's own data.
6. Search a route/date and open a trip.
7. Open seat selection. Explain that Fetch/AJAX calls api/seats.php every 4 seconds.
8. Select a seat and continue. Explain server-side transaction + UNIQUE(trip_id, seat_id).
9. Choose Card, USSD or Bank Transfer.
10. First demonstrate a FAILED simulated payment. Show the failed row in the payments table.
11. Then demonstrate a SUCCESSFUL simulated payment. Show booking status confirmed, payment success and ticket row in MySQL.
12. Open confirmation and e-ticket.
13. Open booking history and dashboard.
14. Log in as admin and demonstrate passenger management.

IMPORTANT: This is a simulated payment system. Do not enter real card information.
